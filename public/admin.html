Post a permanent announcement

Edit content/content.json, add an item under announcements, set its visibility to public, and commit to main.

Publish a permanent lab sheet

Upload the PDF into source-materials/, add its exact filename to the relevant lab entry, and set visibility to public.

Schedule or hide content

Use scheduled with a future releaseAt time, or use draft to keep an item out of the deployed website.

Public means public: anyone can download a file once it is published. A public repository also exposes files committed to it, even when the website marks them as drafts.
